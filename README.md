## 🧭 How I build:

- **Start with the product:** Understand the users, business goals, and workflows before choosing the architecture.
- **Give AI useful context:** Connect models to relevant information through retrieval pipelines and well-defined tools.
- **Keep workflows clear:** Define agent responsibilities, expected outputs, and where human review is needed.
- **Connect the whole experience:** Bring frontend interfaces, backend services, AI integrations, and cloud deployment together.
